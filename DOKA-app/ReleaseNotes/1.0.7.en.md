- **Right ⌥ and right ⌘ as standalone keys.** Assign the right Option or Command key to any command in Shortcuts: press and release it in the field. Push-to-talk works by holding it, other commands by a short press. Combinations like ⌥ + letter, ⌘C or ⌘-click keep working as usual and don't trigger anything.
- **The lock only when recording is locked.** The lock icon appears after you lock push-to-talk, not from the first second of every dictation.
- **The lip mirror appears in one piece.** The plate with the camera frame slides out from the screen edge as a whole — no separate strip before it and no bounce.
