- **Go hands-free without stopping dictation.** While holding push-to-talk, press **Control + Shift + Space**: release all keys and recording continues. Press the same shortcut again to pause, then again to resume. Finish and paste with your start/stop shortcut or another push-to-talk press; Esc cancels the entire dictation.
- **One command, your shortcut.** Customize “Lock / pause” in Shortcuts. It only captures the shortcut during dictation, leaving regular Space available for typing. Paused audio is not recorded and the timer stops; the panel and menu bar show the state. Recordings with a pause are not saved for lip training, keeping audio and video in sync.
- **Notice updates even with the window closed.** A dot on the DOKA menu bar icon and “Update DOKA” / “What’s new” menu items appear when an update is ready. Checks are more frequent, including at launch and after sleep; notes for the installed version remain available in About DOKA after restarting.
- **Easier agent setup.** Click outside the port field to apply it and dismiss focus. The activity log initially shows 20 calls with a button to reveal more. Log captions and connection field sizes have been improved.
