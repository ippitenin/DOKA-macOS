- **DOKA now updates itself.** New versions download in the background, an “Update available” banner appears at the bottom of the sidebar, and the Update button relaunches the app on the new version in a couple of seconds. Permissions and your service key are kept. If DOKA is busy recognizing speech or a file, the update waits until it’s done.
- New page “General → About DOKA”: version, update check and update settings.
- The sidebar scrolls when sections don’t fit the window.
